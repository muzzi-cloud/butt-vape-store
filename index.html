Help and information

Frequently asked questions

Customise these answers to match the actual store and applicable local rules.

Is this website ready to take real orders?

No. This is a front-end demonstration. It has no secure backend, inventory, payment gateway, or order management.

Are the product photos and prices accurate?

The images are crops from a generated visual design mockup, and the listed prices are placeholders. Replace them with accurate, authorised product photography and verified information.

Can I connect a payment gateway?

A production build needs a compliant payment provider that permits the product category in your location, secure backend processing, order tracking, and legal review. Provider rules may prohibit some products.

What information should be added before launch?

Real business contact details, product specifications, stock status, delivery and return policies, privacy terms, secure checkout, and any required age verification or compliance notices.

Who can access restricted products?

Only adults who meet the applicable legal age, and only where the products are lawful. Laws may restrict or prohibit the sale, advertising, shipment, or use of vaping products.
